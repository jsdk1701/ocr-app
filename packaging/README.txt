OCR App
=======

Turns scanned PDFs (Gujarati, Hindi, English) into searchable PDFs.

1. Unzip this download completely (the whole folder, not just the app).
2. Open "User-Guide.pdf" and follow "First time you open the app".
3. Start OCR App, click "Add PDF files...", press "Start OCR".
   The searchable copy is saved next to the original as  name-ocr.pdf

No internet connection or account is needed to OCR files.
